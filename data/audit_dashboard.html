The 10 Information Asymmetry Archetypes

Taxonomy framework partitioning 100 messy licensees by corporate structure and data visibility.

ARCHETYPE 01
Statutory Listed Conglomerate
Public parent corporations filing 10-K or consolidated IFRS accounts. Headcount and consolidated revenues are fully verified.
Exemplar: Siemens Healthineers AG (DAX: SHL, €21.7B)
ARCHETYPE 02
Regulated Credit Institution
Commercial banks whose accounts report Net Operating Income rather than CAEN standard turnover. Scrapers misprice at $0.
Exemplar: Banca Transilvania S.A. (8.2B RON operating rev)
ARCHETYPE 03
Foreign Assembly Subsidiary
Large industrial manufacturing plants operated for multinational parents. High headcount with low local billing margin.
Exemplar: Yazaki Romania S.R.L. (3,623 factory staff)
ARCHETYPE 04
Transfer-Pricing EMEA Hub
Regional billing centers booking tens of billions in revenue with a few thousand corporate campus staff. Pricing trap!
Exemplar: Google Ireland Limited (€86.6B / 4.8k seats)
ARCHETYPE 05
Statutory-Exempt Micro Craft
Artisan retailers and workshops filing micro-balance sheets with P&L legally withheld. Solved via unit-economic proxies.
Exemplar: Margot Bakery (£490k) & Thunemann KG (€1.35M)
ARCHETYPE 06
Ultra-Secretive Foundation Titan
Global luxury giants owned by private non-profit foundations filing zero public accounts. Solved via physical output volume.
Exemplar: Rolex SA ($11.5B wholesale / 14k staff)
ARCHETYPE 07
High-Velocity VC Venture
Tech companies whose revenue grows 10x in 12 months. Static registry filings lag by 2 years. Solved via dev adoption velocity.
Exemplar: Anysphere / Cursor ($150M ARR run-rate)
ARCHETYPE 08
Statutory Zero Wealth Shell
Family asset holding entities legally barred from commercial trading. Balance sheet assets ≠ commercial revenue.
Exemplar: Steyn Holding S.A., SPF (Statutory €0 revenue)
ARCHETYPE 09
Corporate Spin-Off / Carve-Out
Businesses spun out of global parents whose registration status changes rapidly. Requires tracing SEC Form 15-12G.
Exemplar: WK Kellogg Co ($2.7B / 3.3k staff, deregistered)
ARCHETYPE 10
Insolvent Zombie / Ambiguous Name
Bankrupt estates or polysemic trade names with zero distinguishing metadata. Deliberately refused to prevent bad debt.
Exemplar: Wirecard AG (Insolvent) & Apex Group (Refused)
